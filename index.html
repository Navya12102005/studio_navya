A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before we
begin.

Can we start with just one service?+

Absolutely. A single project is a good way to see how we work together. We can build a broader plan when it makes sense for your business.

What will I receive each month?+

Your proposal names the exact content, campaigns, revisions and reporting included. The service descriptions above show example scopes; we confirm yours before work begins.

Will the content still sound like me?+

We begin with your voice, reference material and audience. You review the direction and approve content in a shared calendar before it is published.

How do you measure progress?+

We agree on a baseline and relevant measures, such as qualified enquiries, website clicks, engagement quality or email conversions. Results depend on your offer, audience, budget and consistency; there are no guaranteed numbers.

Are the portfolio projects client work?+

The current portfolio contains clearly labelled concept briefs. They demonstrate proposed strategies and deliverables, rather than completed client campaigns or verified performance results.

What do you need from me to get started?+

Your goals, brand assets, existing content and a conversation about your audience. If execution needs account access, we agree on the appropriate access during onboarding.
